About PSM

We're the people who turn problems into products.

Problem Solving Mind is a product-first technology company. Young, serious, engineering-driven — and built around one conviction about how to do this work.

Why we exist

Because most software is sold, not solved for.

Businesses are drowning in tools that were sold to them — too many systems, too much data entry, too many vendors. What they don't get is software that simply makes the problem go away.

PSM exists to build that software. Starting from the problem, ending with work that's actually simpler.

Product company, not a staffing company

We don't rent engineers to fill your backlog. We build things our name goes on — products first, custom work second, both measured by outcomes.

Small and sharp by design

We keep the team tight so the thinking stays first-rate. Fewer hands, more care — the way serious engineering should be.

Core philosophy
We don't start with technology.
We start with the problem.

That sentence is the whole company. Every product, every solution, every decision traces back to it.

How we think & how we build

Two disciplines, one standard

The way we reason about problems, and the way we turn that reasoning into working software.

How we think

First principles before frameworks. We question the accepted answer, map the real workflow, and ask what success actually looks like before a single line of code.

  • The problem owns the conversation — until tech is justified, it stays in our back pocket.
  • Small truths first — we'd rather be right about one thing than plausible about everything.

How we build

Real software, shipped in working slices. Automation, AI and integrations only where they remove actual work. Designed for the humans who'll use it daily.

  • Working beats impressive — we ship the smallest thing that genuinely works, then let usage steer.
  • Built to keep improving — every system we hand over is a capability, not a dead end.
The people

Engineers, thinkers, and product people

A tight team of builders who'd rather be known for one excellent thing than ten average ones. Founders work on the products — they don't just manage them.

Vision

A company built to become global.

PSM is young today. It's meant to become a serious, worldwide product company — the kind whose name on a platform means it was engineered properly, for everyone who shares the problem.

  • Products with reach. Platforms used beyond one engagement, one market, one language.
  • A reputation built on outcomes. Known less for what we promise and more for what works.
  • Every engagement compounding. The bench grows with each build — that's how a small team becomes a big company.
